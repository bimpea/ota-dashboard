### Major players

- **Booking Holdings** is merging the hotel-room sales arms of Booking.com, Agoda
  and Priceline into a single operation running on **Agoda's engine** — a major
  consolidation of supply-side infrastructure across the group. Q2 2026: room
  nights **325M** (+5% YoY), gross bookings **$51B** (+9%).
- **Expedia Group** Q2 2026 revenue **$4.32B** (+14% YoY); full-year gross
  bookings guidance raised to **$129.5B–$130.8B**. Also this year: acquired
  **Layla** (AI-native trip planning, founded 2023) and announced a **CLEAR**
  partnership on top of the earlier Uber tie-up, positioning itself past booking
  into the whole trip.
- **Agoda** launched the **Partner Portal** on 17 Aug 2026, retiring the Yield
  Control System; uses AI to condense guest reviews into summaries.
- **Airbnb × Tripadvisor** — announced 11 Aug, still the most consequential
  distribution story in the window. A selection of Tripadvisor/Viator tours,
  activities and attractions becomes bookable on Airbnb later this year, and
  Airbnb drops its build-your-own Experiences supply strategy. Tripadvisor now
  distributes through Airbnb alongside Booking.com and Expedia.
- **Airbnb host fee change** (CNBC, 22 Aug): by **15 Sep** all U.S. hosts move to
  a single **15.5%** host service fee with no separate guest service fee at
  checkout. Hosts are frustrated; some are expected to raise listed prices to
  avoid earning less per booking. Directly relevant to Airbnb readers of this
  dashboard.
- **Airbnb hotel supply**: distribution deal with boutique operator **Lark
  Hotels** adds 75+ U.S. properties, including AWOL, Blind Tiger and Bluebird —
  continuing Airbnb's drift toward conventional lodging distribution.
- **Fora Travel**, now at a ~$1B valuation, is being framed as an
  advisor-network challenger to Expedia and Booking.com (Forbes).

### Regulatory

- **Regulation (EU) 2024/1028** applies from **20 May 2026**: platforms must
  transmit **monthly per-listing activity data** to national single digital entry
  points, and hosts need a registration ID displayed on every listing. Substantive
  caps stay local, so limits still diverge sharply by city.
- **United States**: Austin's amendments require platform cooperation — license
  display, **removal of unlicensed listings from 1 July 2026**, and hotel
  occupancy tax collected by the platform.
- **Indonesia** is tightening how villas, apartments and guesthouses may be listed
  on Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda and other OTAs.
- Broad 2026 direction across every major market: more registration requirements,
  stricter zoning, wider platform-collected tax.
- No DMA ruling specific to travel platforms surfaced in this window.

- **American Airlines** raised standard Admirals Club membership up to **75% to
