## Travel / OTA news gathered (ready to paste into the dashboard)

### Booking.com / Booking Holdings
- Q2 2026 beat: higher profit on rising revenue. Domestic US travel is
  carrying results while cross-border demand stays uneven and input costs rise.
- Reports positive ROI on AI spend via narrow use cases — Priceline's "Penny"
  assistant and Agoda customer-service cost savings — rather than broad rollouts.
- Effected a 25-for-1 stock split on 2026-04-02.

### Expedia Group
- Q2 2026 revenue $4.32B, +14% YoY. Raised FY2026 gross bookings guidance to
  $129.5B–$130.8B.
- Acquired Layla (AI trip planning) earlier in 2026; CarTrawler acquisition
  expected to close H2 2026, positioning Expedia as a "one-stop shop for B2B travel."

### Airbnb
- **Leadership change (Sept 1):** Pepijn Rijvers — ex-Tripadvisor and ex-Booking
  Holdings — replaces Dave Stephenson as chief business officer, covering
  short-term rentals, enterprise, global markets and the growing hotels offering.
- Shares jumped 17% after Q2 earnings.
- Led a $58M Series C in WeRoad (group travel).

### Tripadvisor / Viator
- **Airbnb partnership (announced Aug 11):** Tripadvisor tours, activities and
  attractions will appear on Airbnb later in 2026. Tripadvisor/Viator supplies
  ~425,000 experiences globally. Signals Airbnb dropping its build-your-own
  experiences strategy in favour of third-party supply.

### Agoda
- Launched the Partner Portal on 2026-08-17, replacing the Yield Control System.

### AI / agentic booking
- Google expanded agentic hotel booking into AI Mode chat in the US — travelers
  can find, compare and book inside the conversational interface. Launch partners
  include Booking.com, Expedia, Hilton, Marriott, IHG, Choice, Wyndham, Priceline,
  Hotels.com and Trip.com. Google maintains it has "no intention of becoming an OTA."
- Adoption gap is the story: only ~2% of US consumers say they'd use a fully
  autonomous booking agent, while ~80% of travel executives plan to deploy them
  at scale. AI is improving planning efficiency without lifting booking conversion.
- Skift's State of Travel 2026 frames discovery as shifting from
  search-scroll-compare to ask-shortlist-decide.

### Funding & M&A
- Q1 2026 travel funding deal volume hit a record low; Q2 stayed slow, skewed to
  seed and Series B. M&A is now the dominant growth mechanism.
- Incumbents (Expedia, Booking, Amadeus) are targeting "agent-ready" B2B services.
- Other deals: Juniper acquired Deem (corporate travel tech); Lighthouse acquired
  Hotelrank.ai.

### Regulatory
- **EU Regulation 2024/1028** on short-term rental transparency took effect
  2026-05-20: interoperable registration and data-sharing systems, platforms must
  verify and display registration numbers, monthly data reporting via national
  "single digital entry points." Opt-in/opt-out for Member States — but any state
  running an STR registry must do it through this framework.
- The European Commission's comprehensive housing strategy is due **9 September**
  and is expected to give national and local governments more explicit latitude to
  curb short-term rentals and second homes.

### Industry calendar
- Skift Global Forum, 22–24 September 2026, North Javits Center NYC. 1,200+ leaders;
  CEOs of Airbnb, Booking Holdings, Hilton, Expedia and Uber plus OpenAI chairman
  Bret Taylor expected.
